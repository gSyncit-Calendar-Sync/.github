## First Look at gSyncit

gSyncit is for people who already keep Outlook and other calendars they own, and want one named bridge. It gives you a contact bridge, an Outlook desk, and a short path back to folders you already use. It does not replace Outlook. It sits beside Outlook and keeps the link close.

Work stays on accounts you own and already authorize. Extra accounts wait for their own title.
